Ideas

1. Central ideas:





Representing dysphoria as a tangible entity to remove some of the abstract aspect from it, making it more comprehensible.





Dysphoria is such a hard thing to explain and even more complicated to imagine so representing it as a tangible thing gives us a lens that we can use to explore the various aspects of it and how it can affect someone.



Anxiety, self doubt and isolation and how the Shadow exerts these over someone are all touched upon (even if subtly) in the story

Charting Your Story

1. Plot structure:





Three act structure, ending in a tragic trope twist





Beginning:





Opal's (protagonist) Vial gets stolen by a regular at the bar that has been notably tense in their last few visits, acting irregularly and focusing heavily on Opal's actions and routines.



Middle:





Throughout the troubles and puzzles Opal resolves, they learn about the small but important effects their action and presence have on people around them, being able to find a new light in their actions and how they handle the situations that follow in the story.





As Opal tries to manage the issues that the shadow presents within themselves, stress and anxiety start to make them doubt in everything they're experiencing, making the decisions and behaviours of Opal increasingly erratic and even agressive bringing difficulty to their situation.





End:





All the information gathered and distance traveled by Opal (both physical and mental) take a heavy toll on them, making the confrontation with the thief highly agressive and making a big enough scene that makes Opal get wrongly accused of insanity and being forcibly admitted into the mental asylum the antagonist works at.

Characters and Motivations 

1. Main characters:





Protagonist:





Name: Opal



Traits: 





Crafty: Worked in the bar from a young age and inherited it from their parents, learning to think outside the box to fix and extend the life of various items and trinkets.



Charismatic: Working as a bartender has given Opal the chance of speaking to all kinds of people  and hear their drunken troubles, making them learn how to read people's feelings by their expression and body language, making them able to approach almost everyone with a calm and trustworthy demeanor.



Self-isolating: Driven by the constant trouble of keeping their Shadow at bay, Opal tends to keep mostly surface-level relationships with people.



Physical Description: Lanky, skeletic and tired  face (visible cheekbones and very slim/notorious eye bags), average height with medium length hair (Mikasa bob type, should have a nice contrast with Shadow's elongated and dark appearance), expressive eyes (they work for notorious contrast with the rest of the face, and good for expressing distress)



Motivations: When the Vial gets stolen their life takes a massive turn in the need to get it back as this contrasts the mostly boring and solitary life they tried to lead as a simple bartender, going from just trying to pass day by day to having to actively seek out help from people they dont consider close and getting surprised when they agree to help.





Their sole motivation is to find the thief to get their Vial back and return to their usual life and spend the rest of their days in peace.



Antagonist (Shadow):





Name: Onyx



Traits:





Draining appearances: Onyx's various appearances throughout the story get more frequent as Opal gets more agitated and anxious while also making their state worse with teasing comments and reinforcing Opal's insecurities.



Corporeal only to Opal: Onyx is as tangible as any other NPC to our main character, but it can only be seen and heard by them (think Johhny Silverhand from Cyberpunk 2077), since its essentially the embodying of feelings made tangible, it cant interact with anything other than Opal.



Physical Description: Almost identical to Opal but uncanny enough to be creepy.





Elongated features to mimic the different angles that can cast a shadow.





This applies to its hair too, being way longer than Opal's and casting a "shadow" or "cloak" of its own due to its length.



Visible and anatomically incorrect ribcage: Since its essentially a shadow we'll play with more transparent tones for its "skin" and make its ribcage visible to show its incorrect even to its core, with the ribcage being twisted and crooked to reflect the incorrect "copy" of Opal.



Uncanny expressions: Having a twisted grin or extreme pout when emoting helps sell the emotion wreck that Onyx is.



Still eyes: In direct contrast to Opal's eyes, being still, "dead" and way smaller than Opals, serving mostly to give the face an uncanny look.



Motivations:





Opposition to the protagonist: Onyx as a concept isn't inherently evil nor is it its goal to actively oppose the protagonist, its a consequence of its existence bringing insecurities and stress factors to life, being tangible and constantly looming over Opal.





Its motivations arent really apparent to Opal, it seems to just appear to make Opal feel as bad as it can and can have teasing attitudes when things are going Opal's way.



Antagonist (Thief):





Name: Grace



Traits:





Manipulative: Grace's experiments and lack of care about what happens to other Vial users has made them efficient at lying and covering up their activities.





Working at the mental asylum from which they steal supplies gives them access to substances to cover their tracks by tricking or drugging unsuspecting people.



Physical Description:





Short height with well kept hair  (in a bun), wearing a victorian era style nurse/staff dress with a white apron.





Inexpressive face, maintaining a serious demeanor all the time due to an absolute lack of caring



Motivations: Researching the Vial to figure out how to produce more of them that work for anyone that needs them, since they are quite rare and unique objects "bound" to their owners.



Opposition to the protagonist: Grace looks at their thievery as "necessary" evil to do good and be able to research the Vial and its nature and this directly clashes against Opal's need for the Vial to help supress Spot's effects.





Grace doesn't have anything against Opal specifically, they just see their Vial as necessary for their research and doesn't care about the consequences that may surge for Opal due to the lack of their Vial.
